The Clockwork Archive of Dr. Evelyn Thorne

Welcome, Junior Archivists! Renowned British linguist and clockmaker Dr. Evelyn Thorne has mysteriously vanished inside her private Victorian research facility, The Clockwork Archive.

To protect her latest chronological discoveries, she sealed the main laboratory vault with a 4-digit mechanical lock and distributed the clues across 4 thematic security chambers (18 challenges in total).

🇬🇧
British English Protocol: Dr. Thorne's archive only accepts standard British English spelling and grammar (e.g., realise, colour, favourite, centre, catalogue).
